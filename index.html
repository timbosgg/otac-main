Oceania Tactical

Oceania Tactical (OTAC) is a semi-realistic Arma Reforger milsim unit focused on professional execution and immersive operations. Built around a single infantry platoon, we aim to provide a grounded milsim experience without overcomplication.

  • Based in ANZ

    An Oceania unit running all operations on AEST-friendly schedules, with members welcome from any time zone.

  • Balanced realism

    Structured and professional without being overbearing, with realism set at a level that stays fun and approachable.

  • Every role matters

    A welcoming community where operations are built around the full platoon, from the rifle sections through to support roles.

Roles

1-1 is an infantry platoon of three 8-man rifle sections and a direct fire support weapons (DFSW) section, operating under a single chain of command. Every mission is built for the platoon to carry out. The specialist and support roles work alongside it, handling command, communications, casualty care and aviation so the sections can stay focused on the objective.

Mission style

Our missions are built around a light infantry platoon supported by aviation elements. They range from single-night raids to multi-week campaigns with a continuing story. We put an emphasis on variety, giving mission creators freedom in how they set objectives and leadership freedom in how they carry them out.

  • Air assault operations
  • Direct action raids
  • Reconnaissance missions
  • Seize and hold objectives
  • Game Master-run operations
  • Flexible objective structures
