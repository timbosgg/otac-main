Oceania Tactical

OTAC operates as a semi-realistic milsim unit focused on professional execution and immersive operations. We aim to provide a grounded, immersive milsim experience without overcomplication.

  • Based in ANZ

    Operated by Australian and New Zealand players across the Oceania region running on AEST-friendly schedules.

  • Semi-realistic

    Structured and professional without being overbearing. We balance realism with fun and approachability.

  • Community focus

    A tight-knit unit where leadership is earned and every member plays a meaningful role in each operation.

Mission style

Our missions are built around a light infantry platoon supported by aviation assets, with an emphasis on variety to enable flexible objectives and execution by leadership and mission creators.

  • Air assault operations
  • Direct action raids
  • Reconnaissance missions
  • Special operations-style raids
  • Flexible objective structures
